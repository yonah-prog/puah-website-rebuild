## How the forms send (important before launch)
The site is static, with no server. So for now, submitting a form opens the visitor's email app with the message already addressed to the right inbox, and the page shows a thank-you note. This works today on Vercel. **Recommended next step:** connect the forms to a server-side endpoint, such as a Vercel serverless function plus an email service, or the Resource Library's existing submissions system. Then messages send silently and nobody needs a mail app. That's a one-line change per inbox in the `sendForm` function.
